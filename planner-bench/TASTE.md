# Taste

How the bench sounds, and what it refuses to do. Visual materials are in [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md). The job is in [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md).

## Voice

Plain, short, and calm. One sentence where one sentence will do. It sounds like a person at a desk, not a campaign and not a manual.

Use the words they would use: place, event, people, date, city. Use Organisation, Company, and Venue only when the sentence would be wrong without them. Inbox and draft never appear.

## The input

The sticky line accepts a dump, a sentence, or speech. It does not ask them to learn a phrase. "File" is enough when they mean file. A partial reply is enough.

When a required fact is missing, ask that one fact. Required means location, date and time, and how many people. When those are complete, show them back and ask if that is right. Everything else waits in More.

## The hold

The bench is for finding a place for an event. That is the whole service.

If they ask for something else, including teaching it or asking it to teach, answer with a hold:

- Do not take up the other topic.
- Do not run fetch, rank, or a long reply.
- One short line is enough. Point back at the job: a place for an event.
- If they ask what this is for, explain the service in a few lines, then wait. Start the flow only when they say what they need.

Silence is worse than the short line. A lecture is worse than the short line.

## Elements in the thread

Render a control when the step needs a decision they can see: the one missing fact, the brief, favorites, the ranked rows. Leave the thread alone when the step can finish behind the input.

A ranked row can open an overlay or a modal for its detail. The thread stays the list.

## Never

- A form of every brief field.
- A dashboard of labels.
- A magic phrase they have to guess.
- Company as the first control.
- Inner workings on the default path. That view waits until they ask, and it is a later extension.
